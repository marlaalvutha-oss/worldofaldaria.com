The Rite of Returning

A World of Aldaria Story

When necromancer Marla al-Vutha arrives in the village of Millbrook to perform the ancient Rite of Returning, she expects a peaceful ceremony honoring the dead. Instead, she finds a desecrated cemetery, restless spirits, a murderous Harvest Guardian, and an alarming number of homicidal vegetables. With Magnes at her side, Marla must restore the boundary between life and death before the sacred ceremony can begin. A wickedly funny tale of magic, mayhem, remembrance, and the passionate affirmation that life goes on.

The Warlock Affair

A Tale from the World of Aldaria

What should have been a straightforward job retrieving a magical artifact turns into something far more complicated when Magnes Magnessun discovers a dead warlock and a very much alive succubus. Bound by powerful magic and desperate for freedom, the beautiful Meridiana presents Magnes with a temptation he never anticipated. Filled with dangerous magic, sharp wit, unexpected connections, and plenty of heat, The Warlock Affair proves that sometimes the greatest adventure isn't the one you were hired for.
